## Executive summary

FantasyMax is a **Next.js + Supabase** “historical and social layer” for a Yahoo fantasy league: stats, H2H, writeups, media, password-gated access, and heavy use of **AI-generated recaps/reviews**. The **core feature set is largely shipped** (see `features.json` and `docs/ROADMAP.md`). The main open strategic gap is **auth/RLS vs shareable read-only mode**, and the largest **data** gap is **trades (0 imported)**.

## Security & access model (critical for “production”)

The codebase documents a **shareable** deployment:

- **Password gate** (Phase 9 in `features.json`): `password-gate`, `gate-page`, `gate-api` — pass.
- Widespread **`createAdminClient()`** and **`BYPASS_AUTH`** / **RLS not enforced** for this mode, called out as **High** in `docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md`.

**Conclusion:** Appropriate for a **trusted league + shared password** and obfuscated URL; **not** equivalent to per-member Supabase auth + RLS. Choose intentionally:

- **Friends-only URL + gate:** keep current pattern; harden operationally (password rotation, rate limits on gate API, key hygiene).
- **True member product:** follow V2 “Production ready” in `KNOWN_ISSUES` — `createClient`, RLS, invites, remove dev bypasses.

## Money / “gambling-adjacent” ideas — safe framing (not legal advice)

Real-money wagering, credit, and payouts in-app bring **compliance and dispute** risk. A common **fantasy-appropriate** pattern:

1. **Virtual league currency** — not redeemable, reset or soft-carry for bragging rights only; e.g. “spot everyone 50” as **game balance**, not a deposit.
2. **Props / predictions on league outcomes** — all prices/scores from **synced stats**; leaderboard in-app.
3. **Cash settlement outside the app** — treasurer, Venmo, draft night; app shows **liability for fun**, not custodial money.

**Existing precedent in repo:** weekly high score earnings are modeled as a **$50/week league rule** in logic such as `src/lib/supabase/queries/earnings.ts` (formula + display), not in-app payment rails.

**If** considering anything real-money adjacent: **stop and get legal review** before building.
