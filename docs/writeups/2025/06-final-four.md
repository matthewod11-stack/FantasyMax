# This Week: The Final Four

Laces Out (8) vs. ODEEZUS (4)

Laces Out caught lightning last week. Can they do it again? Trevor Lawrence looked like 2021 Trevor Lawrence. Nico Collins is healthy. Ashton Jeanty might actually be the best RB in this matchup despite Bijan's prowess.

ODEEZUS escaped by 3 points. Dak needs to be better than 11.96. Bijan Robinson might carry this team to the championship on his back, but they can't afford another squeaker against a hot 8-seed.

Joe Buck Yourself (2) vs. theACEboogie (3)

Rematch potential of titans. Drake Maye vs. Jared Goff. Amon-Ra St. Brown vs. Christian McCaffrey. Two of the most complete rosters left standing.

Joe Buck has been here before, posted the second-best regular season, and St. Brown is a cheat code. But ACEboogie just hung 193.27 on a good team. If Goff stays hot and CMC keeps getting volume, this could go either way.

Snow in the forecast for some of these games. That's a wildcard.
