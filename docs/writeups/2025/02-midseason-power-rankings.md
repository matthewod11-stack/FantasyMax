# The Commish Is Back: Midseason Power Rankings

WELL WELL WELL

THE COMMISH IS BACK

Gentleman, 

Apologies for the lack of communication, to be honest, I just didn’t feel like doing it. 

But here we are, 7 weeks into the 14 week season and I said to myself, I got to give the people what they want!

Some things I’ve noticed so far.  Waiver moves are WAY down this year.  As we all know there is absolutely 0 people on the waiver.  Losing tyreek hill and then replacing him with Kendre Miller  (avg 3 points a game) doesn’t make for a fun nor competitive experience.  I’m considering dropping a roster spot (or maybe 2) next season to make things a little more interesting week to week.  As it stands now, its like draft the best team you can, hope your guys don’t go down, when they do, replace them with a scrub who you hope has 1 catch for 60 yards a td as their only touch of the game.  Not really into that. Ofcourse, less teams means more players in the pool, but I don’t want to go down that road just yet. 

Another thought I’m continuing to have is around transitioning the league from yahoo to sleeper.  It’s just a more technically advanced tool.  In particular I like their feature where you get 2 points per win, if your team is above the weekly average score (you get 1 point if you lose that game I believe).  If you win and you score below the avg, then you only get 1 point.  This makes those 98 to 94 wins less impactful, and those narrow 161 to 159 sting less. 

Will continue monitoring and would welcome your insights.
