# 11 Years in the Meat Grinder

Standalone 2026 kickoff infographic for the League of Degenerates.

The page contains a frozen, credential-free snapshot of FantasyMax history from the linked production Supabase project. It intentionally does not connect to Supabase at runtime.

## Data Notes

- Seasons: 2015–2025
- Inaugural season: 12 teams
- 2016 onward: 14 teams
- The snapshot deduplicates two extra 2025 team rows that repeat final ranks 8 and 14.
- “40% of seasons” remains commissioner trash talk in the kickoff email. The database contains 11 completed seasons, making four championships 36.4% of loaded history.
